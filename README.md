# MyDaily Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 22, 2026

## 1. Introduction

MyDaily ("the App") is a personal productivity application that helps users manage tasks, projects, daily activities, and guitar practice. It can also connect to compatible devices, including SmartTinyDevice, to support connected features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information MyDaily may collect, how it is used, how it is stored, and the choices available to users.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, MyDaily may process the following information:

### Account Information

When you create an account or sign in, the App may process your account identifier and authentication information through its authentication provider.

### Tasks, Projects, and User Content

The App may store information you create, such as task titles, descriptions, priorities, project details, comments, guitar practice sessions, and practice progress.

### Device and Application Information

The App may access limited device or application information needed for compatibility, troubleshooting, and feature functionality.

### Bluetooth and Connected Devices

If you use compatible SmartTinyDevice hardware, the App may use Bluetooth to discover, connect to, and communicate with the device. Bluetooth access is used to support the requested connected features.

### Notifications and Background Operations

The App may schedule notifications and perform background operations to support reminders, synchronization, and other enabled functionality.

The exact information processed depends on the permissions granted and the features enabled by the user.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed by MyDaily may be used to:

* Authenticate users and maintain their accounts.
* Save and synchronize tasks, projects, and practice records.
* Display personalized productivity information and progress.
* Connect to compatible Bluetooth devices.
* Deliver reminders and notifications.
* Maintain application functionality, security, and reliability.
* Troubleshoot technical issues and improve the user experience.

## 4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

MyDaily uses Appwrite services for backend functionality, which may include authentication and storage of application data.

Information processed by Appwrite is subject to the applicable service configuration and Appwrite's privacy and security practices.

The App may also rely on platform services and software libraries to provide features such as Bluetooth connectivity, notifications, and authentication.

Data may be processed by these service providers as necessary to deliver the requested functionality. Their access and processing are subject to the applicable service arrangements and privacy policies.

MyDaily does not sell users' personal information.

## 5. Data Sharing

MyDaily does not share personal information with third parties for their independent advertising purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the App, provide requested functionality, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

MyDaily does not intentionally make private task or project content publicly available.

## 6. Permissions

Depending on the device and features used, MyDaily may request permissions related to:

* Bluetooth connectivity and nearby devices.
* Notifications and reminders.
* Background operations required for supported features.

Permissions are used to support the relevant application functionality. Users can review and manage available permissions through their device settings. Disabling a permission may limit the associated feature.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Account information and associated application data may be retained while an account remains active or as necessary to provide the service.

Users who want to delete their account and associated data can contact the developer using the contact information below.

Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements and any legitimate retention obligations. Data stored in third-party services may require deletion through the relevant service or account-management process.

## 8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, and disclosure.

However, no electronic storage system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Children's Privacy

MyDaily is not designed specifically for children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at the same URL with a revised effective date where appropriate.
