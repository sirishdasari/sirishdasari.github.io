MyDaily Account and Data Deletion

MyDaily users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Send an email to the MyDaily developer using the contact address below. Include your registered account email or another identifier that allows us to locate your account. Do not include your password.

Request Account Deletion

What happens next?

After receiving your request, we will verify account ownership and process the deletion of your account and associated data in accordance with our privacy policy and applicable legal requirements.

Data deletion and retention

Account-associated data will be deleted unless certain information must be retained for legitimate legal or security reasons. Any retained data and the applicable retention period will be explained to the user where required.
