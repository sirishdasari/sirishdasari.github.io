MyDaily Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

MyDaily is a personal productivity application for managing tasks, projects, daily activities, and guitar practice, including supported connected-device features.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, MyDaily may process account identifiers and authentication information; tasks, project details, comments and guitar-practice records you create; limited device and application information; and Bluetooth or notification information needed for features you enable.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to authenticate users, save and synchronize content, show personalized progress, connect compatible devices, deliver reminders, maintain security and reliability, and troubleshoot issues.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

MyDaily uses Appwrite services for backend functionality, which may include authentication and application-data storage. Platform services and software libraries may also support Bluetooth, notifications and authentication. These providers process information as needed to deliver the relevant features, under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

MyDaily does not sell users' personal information.

4. Data Sharing

MyDaily does not share personal information with third parties for their independent advertising purposes. Information may be processed by service providers to operate the app, provide requested features, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

5. Permissions

Depending on the features used, MyDaily may request Bluetooth or nearby-device, notification, and background-operation permissions. You can manage available permissions in your device settings; disabling them may limit related features.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Account information and related app data may be retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact dasarisirish@gmail.com. We will verify the request and handle it in accordance with applicable requirements and legitimate retention obligations. Data held by third-party providers may require deletion through their relevant processes.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse and disclosure. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

MyDaily is not designed specifically for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. Contact the developer if you have a concern.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as features, data practices or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy, data-handling or deletion questions, contact the MyDaily developer at dasarisirish@gmail.com.
